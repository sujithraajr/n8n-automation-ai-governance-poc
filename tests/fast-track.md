# Test 1 - Fast Track

## Purpose

Validate that a low-risk, non-sensitive process can be routed to the **FAST_TRACK** governance path.

## Test data

**Requester name:** Sujith Raaj  
**Team:** Marketing Operations  
**Process name:** Weekly campaign performance summary  
**Current process:** Every Friday, campaign performance data is copied from a spreadsheet, summarized, and shared with the marketing team. The process does not contain customer-level or sensitive information.  
**Frequency:** Weekly  
**Minutes per run:** 25  
**Systems involved:** Google Sheets, email, Slack  
**Data sensitivity:** No sensitive data  
**Decision impact:** Low  
**Will the automation use AI?:** No  
**Desired outcome:** Automatically collect the weekly figures, create a consistent summary, and send it to the team to reduce repetitive manual reporting.

## Expected result

**Governance route:** `FAST_TRACK`

## Why

The process is low impact, does not involve sensitive data, and does not require AI to make a business decision.
